Empowering Egyptian Artisans

In Egypt, many skilled women produce high-quality handmade carpets, yet remain excluded from international markets due to structural barriers. Exora bridges this gap.

We actively facilitate and secure the entire transaction. By introducing a built-in escrow system and handling logistics, we provide international buyers with a trusted sourcing channel, while giving artisans the global reach they deserve.

  • No bank requirements for artisans.
  • Global demand connected directly to local supply.

A Process Built on Trust

Seamless integration of verification, escrow, and logistics.

1. Verified Artisans

Artisans and NGOs are locally verified to ensure authenticity of proper handmade production.

2. Escrow Payment

Buyers place orders. Funds are safely held in escrow by Exora, removing risk for both parties.

3. Quality Check

Pre-shipment inspection confirms product quality matches exactly what was promised.

4. Delivery

Exora manages logistics. Payment is released to artisans only upon successful delivery.
